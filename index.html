About & How to Use
What is this
📝

প্রশ্নপত্র Maker creates Bengali exam papers that print two identical copies on one A4 landscape sheet. Cut the dotted centre line — two students each get one set.

🤖 AI OCR Prompt
✨

Use this specific prompt with an AI assistant (like Gemini or ChatGPT) to accurately digitize printed question papers into the QPF format.

🔒 100% Offline — No Server
🛡

Nothing leaves your device. No server, no login, no analytics. Runs entirely in your browser RAM. Works offline after first load.

💾

RAM usage: Typically 5–15MB per paper. Images add more. No concern on any modern device.

📦 Libraries Used
⚙

JSZip 3.10Google Fonts (CDN)Tiro Bangla (local)

JSZip is only used for ODT export. PDF uses the browser's own print engine. Zero other dependencies.

🤖 OCR Flow (Photo → Paper)
1

Take a photo of a printed question paper. Open Gemini or ChatGPT.

2

Upload the photo. Copy and paste the AI OCR Prompt provided above.

3

Copy the AI output. Click "Paste QPF" in the header, paste, click Apply & Fill Form.

4

Review the form, then click Print / PDF. Set A4, Landscape, No margins in the print dialog.

🖨 Print Tips
💡

In Chrome print dialog: A4 · Landscape · Margins: None · Background graphics: ON. Use Chrome or Edge for best results.

For 2-page papers: print both sheets, place side by side, then cut each.
